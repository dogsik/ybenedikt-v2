About Me

I graduated from York University with a Specialized Honours degree in Health Care Management and Policy, where I focused on how public systems work and how they can be made more effective for the people who rely on them.

Over the past several years, I’ve worked across business, community engagement, and leadership roles, which has shaped how I think about cities, local government, and the connection between policy and everyday life.

Professionally, I currently oversee operations and strategic initiatives across several business ventures. My work includes managing business operations at an accounting firm, co-owning and leading marketing and brand development projects for a bakery, and coordinating partnerships, outreach, and operational planning.

I co-own Asters Patisserie, a bakery specializing in delicious desserts and most of all customer experience. Being part of a small business has taught me the importance of listening to people, responding to their needs, and being consistent in maintaining high standards of services.

In 2022, I ran as a Ward 5 Council candidate in Richmond Hill. A big part of that experience was speaking directly with residents and understanding their concerns firsthand. It gave me a practical view of how local government functions and how important it is for people to feel heard and represented.

These experiences have given me practical insights on how communities function, what helps them grow, and how local decisions shape both business and daily life.

I care about supporting local businesses, improving public services, and building communities where people feel genuinely connected and represented.

Quick Facts About Me

  • I studied in the school of Health Care Policy and Management at York University and graduated with a BHS diploma in 2018.
  • My past 10 years I dedicated to studying Canadian health care system and the ways to improve public services: make them more effective and yet reduce their costs.
  • I’m also a business entrepreneur, developing a supply chain of essential consumer goods to the Canadian store chains and pharmacies.

Yuliya is a business entrepreneur who lives and works in ward 5 Richmond Hill. Yuliya has earned a degree in health care management and policy and knows firsthand the deep impact city services have on the quality of life of its residents. Through her work and volunteering in Richmond Hill for many years, Yuliya learned the needs of the community and is here to represent your interests on council.

My Vision

Richmond Hill needs leadership that showcases our city dynamics and is committed to account for the priorities of the residents. It is time to bring in new, fresh leadership and ideas that will focus on what is best for the families living in Richmond Hill.

My Values
