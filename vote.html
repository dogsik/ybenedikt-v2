How to Vote?

Make your voice count in Richmond Hill’s 2026 Municipal Election

1. Make sure you’re eligible to vote

You can vote if you’re:

  • A Canadian citizen
  • At least 18 years old on or before October 26, 2026
  • A resident of Richmond Hill, or own or rent property in Richmond Hill, or are the spouse of someone who does

2. Check that you’re on the Voters’ List

You must be on the Voters’ List to vote

Starting September 1, 2026, you can check, add, or update your voter information online. If you’ve moved, changed your name, or your information has changed since the last election, make sure it is up to date.

Check Your Voter Information →

3. Know who you’ll be voting for

Richmond Hill voters will elect:

  • Mayor
  • Up to 2 Regional & Local Councillors
  • School Board Trustee(s), depending on your school board
If you live in Ward 5, you will be voting for your Ward 5 Councillor.

4. Choose how you want to vote

Key Election Dates — you can vote online OR in person, not both

Vote Anytime 24/7

Online Voting Period

  • Tue, Oct 20 – Mon, Oct 26, 2026
  • Opens 10:00 a.m. (Oct 20) • Closes 8:00 p.m. (Oct 26)
  • From any device and anywhere
  • RichmondHill.ca/RHVotes

Vote securely from your smartphone, tablet, or laptop using your unique Voter ID and PIN from your Voter Information Package (VIP).

Weekend Hours

Advance In-Person Voting

  • Sat, Oct 24 & Sun, Oct 25, 2026
  • Opens 10:00 a.m. • Closes 6:00 p.m. Both days
  • At Any official City Vote Centre
  • RichmondHill.ca/RHVotes

Cast your paper ballot in person ahead of Voting Day. You can vote at ANY Vote Centre across Richmond Hill, not just in Ward 5.

Final Deadline

Official Voting Day

  • Monday, October 26, 2026
  • Opens 10:00 a.m. • Closes 8:00 p.m. Last voting day.
  • All City Vote Centres & Online Portal
  • RichmondHill.ca/RHVotes

Cast your paper ballot in person ahead of Voting Day. You can vote at ANY Vote Centre across Richmond Hill, not just in Ward 5.

5. Watch for your Voter Information Package

Your Voter Information Package (VIP) will arrive in the mail in late September or early October. It will include important information about voting and instructions for casting your ballot.

6. Vote!

Whether you vote online or in person, take a few minutes to learn about the candidates and the issues that matter to you.

Your vote helps shape the future of Richmond Hill.

Election Day: Monday, October 26, 2026
